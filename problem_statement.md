# Business Problem Statement: Customer & Marketing Analytics
## Customer Segmentation, RFM Modeling, Cohort Retention & Lifetime Value (CLV)

## Executive Business Context

In consumer banking, wealth management, asset management, and e-commerce platforms, customer acquisition costs (CAC) continue to escalate. High-growth enterprises cannot achieve sustainable profitability through top-of-funnel acquisition alone; long-term margin expansion requires:
1. **Targeted Customer Segmentation**: Moving beyond crude demographic buckets to behavioral **RFM (Recency, Frequency, Monetary)** scoring that quantifies true commercial value.
2. **Predictive Churn Mitigation**: Detecting behavioral disengagement triggers (e.g. 90-day inactivity thresholds, velocity slowdowns) before customers completely lapse.
3. **Optimizing Marketing Campaign Allocation**: Maximizing Return on Marketing Investment (ROMI) by identifying which customer tiers have the highest conversion elasticity, rather than blasting generic discounts to non-responders.
4. **Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) Governance**: Accurately forecasting future cash flows using probabilistic retention models to determine permissible acquisition expenditure.

---

## Core Business Questions Addressed

1. **Revenue Concentration**: What proportion of total revenue is generated by top-tier customer cohorts ("Champions"), and what are their behavioral hallmarks?
2. **Cohort Decay Patterns**: How does customer retention decay across consecutive 24-month acquisition cohorts, and where does the primary churn cliff occur?
3. **Behavioral Churn Drivers**: What measurable metrics (order frequency, purchase breadths, basket size) separate churned customers from active ones?
4. **Marketing Campaign Lift**: Does customer spend quartile predict propensity to accept marketing offers, and what is the conversion lift of high-spend tiers over baseline?
5. **Algorithmic Outreach Prioritization**: Can classification models (Logistic Regression vs. Random Forest) accurately rank customer propensity to reduce marketing waste?

---

## Technical & Analytical Objectives

- **Dimensional Star Schema**: Design and implement analytical relational tables in SQLite/SQL Server covering transactions, RFM scores, cohort retention, and campaign responses.
- **Advanced SQL Analytics**: Write production queries utilizing `NTILE(5)` quintile scoring, Common Table Expressions (CTEs), and month-index cohort matrices.
- **Statistical Significance Testing**: Validate that demographic and behavioral features (Income, Recency, Spend) have statistically significant separation using Chi-Square and Mann-Whitney U tests ($p < 0.001$).
- **Machine Learning Tournament**: Evaluate Logistic Regression vs. Random Forest for both Churn Prediction and Campaign Propensity, comparing Precision, Recall, F1, and PR-AUC.
- **Power BI Executive Reporting**: Surface actionable customer insights across 4 interactive visual reporting pages.
